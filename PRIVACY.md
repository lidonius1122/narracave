**Principle**
Narracave does not collect personal data for the developer. There are no ads,
no analytics or tracking services and no account with the developer.

**Connection to your Audiobookshelf server**
Narracave connects only to the Audiobookshelf server you enter. It exchanges
the data needed to use it with that server: sign-in data, library and book
data, covers, listening progress and listening sessions (which book, position,
listening time, device name and app version). The operator of that server is
responsible for it and the data stored there. The developer of Narracave has
no access to it unless they operate the server themselves.

**Data on your device**
Credentials are stored in the device keychain. Downloaded audiobooks, covers,
library data, listening history and settings stay on the device and are
removed when you delete the app. With Apple Watch, the app exchanges data
directly between iPhone and watch.

**Imported audiobooks**
Audiobooks imported from the Files app stay on the device and are not sent to
anyone.

**Debug log (optional)**
Off by default. If you turn it on, the app stores diagnostic data locally on
the device (e.g. server address, user name, book titles, errors – never
passwords or tokens). It only leaves the device if you share it yourself.

**Apple**
Purchase, download and updates are handled by Apple's App Store under Apple's
privacy policy. The developer receives crash reports only if you agreed in
your device settings to share them with app developers.

**Your rights**
Under the GDPR you have the right to access, rectification, erasure,
restriction, data portability and objection, and the right to lodge a
complaint with a supervisory authority. As the developer does not collect
personal data through the app, please direct requests about data on your
server to its operator.
